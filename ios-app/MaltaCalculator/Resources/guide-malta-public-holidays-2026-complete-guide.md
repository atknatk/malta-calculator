# Malta Public Holidays 2026

Malta recognises 14 public and national holidays in 2026. Employees working on a public holiday are entitled to **double pay** or a day in lieu.

## National holidays

- **21 March** — Freedom Day
- **7 June** — Sette Giugno
- **8 September** — Victory Day
- **21 September** — Independence Day
- **13 December** — Republic Day

## Public holidays

- **1 January** — New Year's Day
- **10 February** — Feast of St Paul's Shipwreck
- **19 March** — Feast of St Joseph
- **3 April** — Good Friday
- **1 May** — Workers' Day
- **29 June** — Feast of St Peter and St Paul (Mnarja)
- **15 August** — Feast of the Assumption (Santa Marija)
- **8 December** — Immaculate Conception
- **25 December** — Christmas Day

## Falling on a weekend

Under Maltese law, if a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, it is **not** substituted on the following Monday. The day off is simply lost.

## Overtime on holidays

Work performed on a public holiday must be compensated at **double** the ordinary rate, unless a compensatory day off is agreed.
