# Malta SSC Contributions 2026 Explained

**Social Security Contributions (SSC)** are mandatory payroll deductions in Malta. They fund pensions, healthcare and unemployment benefits. Both employee and employer contribute.

## Categories

| Category | Description                  | Weekly Max      |
| -------- | ---------------------------- | --------------- |
| A        | Pensioner                    | €6.62           |
| B        | Part-time                    | €22.94          |
| C (new)  | Full-time — born after 1979  | €55.93          |
| C (old)  | Full-time — born before 1979 | €49.04          |
| D        | Self-employed                | €55.93 / €49.04 |

## Weekly cap

For 2026 the overall weekly cap on insurable earnings is **€559.31**. Earnings above this level do not attract additional SSC.

## Employer obligation

The employer matches the employee contribution under Categories B, C and D. Employers also pay a **Maternity Fund contribution** of 0.3% of insurable earnings.

## How it appears on your payslip

SSC is shown as a single deduction line. The employer's matching contribution does **not** appear on the employee payslip but must be reported via the monthly FS5 return to CFR Malta.
