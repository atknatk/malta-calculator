# Malta Rental Income — 15% Flat Tax

Landlords in Malta can opt to pay a **final withholding tax of 15%** on rental income instead of adding it to their regular taxable income.

## Who can opt in?

The 15% flat rate is available to:

- **Residential lets** registered with the Housing Authority
- **Commercial lets** (offices, shops, warehouses)
- **Holiday lets** licensed by the Malta Tourism Authority

## How it works

- Tax is applied to **gross rental income** — no deductions for expenses
- It is a **final** tax — you do not need to declare this income separately on your main tax return
- You must submit **TA24** electronically and pay by **30 April** of the following year

## When is the flat rate worth it?

Consider the flat 15% rate when your marginal income tax rate is higher than 15% and your deductible expenses are low. If your allowable expenses are high (maintenance, agent fees, interest), declaring at the progressive rate may be more efficient.

## Property registration

All residential leases must be registered with the Housing Authority within **10 working days** of signing. Failure to register can result in penalties and disqualification from the 15% scheme.
