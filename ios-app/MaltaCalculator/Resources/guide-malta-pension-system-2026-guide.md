# Malta Pension System 2026 Guide

Malta operates a **three-pillar** pension system designed to provide retirees with income after their working life ends.

## Pillar 1 — State pension

The two-thirds pension is the core state benefit. A qualifying retiree receives up to **two thirds** of their pensionable income, capped at a maximum yearly pension.

- **Retirement age in 2026:** 64 (rising to 65 in coming years)
- **Minimum contributions:** at least 10 years of paid SSC
- **Full pension:** 40 contribution years

## Pillar 2 — Occupational schemes

Voluntary employer-sponsored plans. Not yet mandatory in Malta, but a growing number of employers offer them as part of the benefits package.

## Pillar 3 — Private schemes

Individuals can contribute to qualifying personal retirement schemes and benefit from a **15% income tax credit** (up to a capped amount) on qualifying contributions.

## Special categories

- **Self-employed:** contribute to Class 2 SSC, with specific thresholds
- **Public sector:** typically covered by treasury pensions on top of the state pension
- **Returning migrants:** may qualify for totalised contributions across EU states
