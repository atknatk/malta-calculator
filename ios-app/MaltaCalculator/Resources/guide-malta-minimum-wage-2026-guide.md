# Malta Minimum Wage 2026 Guide

Malta's statutory minimum wage is reviewed annually and adjusted through national Cost of Living Adjustment (COLA) increases.

## 2026 rates

- **18 years and over:** €221.78 per week (approx.)
- **17 years:** €213.54 per week
- **Under 17:** €210.84 per week

These figures apply to full-time employees working a standard 40-hour week.

## COLA impact

In 2026, COLA adds a flat cost-of-living payment on top of basic pay. The full-year COLA entitlement is distributed in quarterly tranches.

- **Q1/Q3:** €121.16
- **Q2/Q4:** €135.10

## Sectoral regulation orders

Certain industries — including hospitality, cleaning, private security and care — have Wage Regulation Orders (WROs) that set minimum rates **above** the national minimum. Always check the applicable WRO for your sector.

## Part-time employees

Part-time workers are entitled to the equivalent hourly rate of the minimum wage, pro-rated against their contracted hours.
