# Malta Mortgage Guide 2026

Buying property in Malta? This guide covers loan-to-value limits, typical interest rates and how the monthly repayment is calculated.

## Loan-to-value (LTV)

Maltese banks typically lend up to **90%** of the property value for a primary residence. For second homes and investment properties the LTV drops to **70–75%**.

## Term

Mortgages can run up to **40 years**, but the loan must typically be fully repaid by age **65**. Most buyers end up with a 25–30 year term.

## Interest rates

As of early 2026 typical variable rates sit between **3.6% and 4.5%**, depending on your LTV and credit profile. Fixed-rate products (3–5 year lock) are also available.

## Monthly repayment — the PMT formula

```
PMT = P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n − 1)
```

Where:

- `P` = principal
- `r` = monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12)
- `n` = total number of monthly payments

> The Malta Calculator mortgage tool uses exact `Decimal` precision for this computation — no Double-precision rounding artefacts.

## Affordability rule of thumb

Keep the monthly repayment below **30%** of your net monthly income. For joint applications, banks combine both salaries but apply the same 30% threshold.

## Next steps

- Get a pre-approval letter (valid for 3 months)
- Engage a notary for the konvenju
- Budget for **stamp duty**, notary fees and surveying — usually another 6–7% of the purchase price
