# Malta Vacation Leave Entitlement 2026

Every full-time employee in Malta is entitled to a statutory minimum of **208 hours** (26 working days) of paid annual leave for 2026.

## How the 208 hours are calculated

- Base entitlement: 192 hours (24 days × 8 hours)
- Plus **additional hours** when public holidays fall on weekends — compensated by law

## Pro-rata for part-time

Part-time employees receive leave pro-rated to their contracted hours. If you work 20 hours per week, your entitlement is **104 hours** (52% of the full-time figure).

## New joiners and leavers

- **Joining mid-year:** entitlement is pro-rated from the start date
- **Leaving mid-year:** unused leave is paid out, or overused leave may be deducted from the final pay slip

## Carry-over

Up to **50% of unused leave** can be carried into the following year if agreed with the employer. The remaining balance must be used within the reference year.

## Sickness during leave

If you fall sick during your vacation and provide a medical certificate, those days are converted to sick leave and the vacation days are returned to your balance.
