# Malta Stamp Duty — Complete Guide 2026

**Stamp duty** is the one-off transfer tax paid by the buyer when property changes hands in Malta.

## Standard rate

The baseline stamp duty rate is **5%** of the higher of the purchase price or market value.

## First-time buyer relief

First-time buyers pay **no stamp duty** on the first **€200,000** of the property value. The remainder is taxed at 5%.

## Second-time buyer relief

Buyers replacing their primary residence can claim a refund of up to **€3,000** on the new purchase — or **€5,000** if at least one spouse has a disability.

## UCA zones (Urban Conservation Areas)

Properties within UCA zones, or Grade 1/2 scheduled buildings, qualify for a reduced rate of **1.5%** (capped at certain price ceilings).

## Gozo property

Buyers purchasing in **Gozo** enjoy a reduced rate of **2%** instead of 5%, as part of the ongoing Gozo fiscal incentive scheme.

## Payment timeline

- **20%** due on signing the konvenju (promise of sale)
- **Balance** due at the final deed of transfer
- Notary collects and remits to CFR Malta

## Example

A first-time buyer purchasing a property for €280,000 pays:

```
Taxable amount = 280,000 − 200,000 = 80,000
Stamp duty     = 80,000 × 5% = 4,000
```
