# Logos

Copied verbatim from `public/`. All three are full-colour PNGs: a gold calculator glyph with a € sign, "Malta" in gold and "Calculator" in near-black.

- `logo.png` (532×112): the horizontal lockup on a transparent background. Use it on light grounds (`background`, `card`) only, since the black "Calculator" disappears on the dark theme. Keep clear space of at least the glyph's corner radius.
- `logo_squaree.png` (600×600): the same lockup centred on a square transparent canvas, for social avatars and directory listings.
- `apple-icon.png` (500×500): the app and touch icon.

There's no SVG or single-ink version yet, so don't recolour or redraw the mark. On dark grounds, set the brand name as live text in Cal Sans instead.
